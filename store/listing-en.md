# Google Play: English

## Название (до 30 символов)
DaSync: Laptop Remote

## Краткое описание (до 80)
Touchpad, screen, files and monitoring for your laptop over home Wi-Fi.

## Полное описание (до 4000)
DaSync turns your phone into a remote for your laptop. Everything works directly over your home Wi-Fi: no accounts, no cloud, no sign-up.

FEATURES
• Touchpad: cursor, clicks, right click, two-finger scroll, drag
• Laptop screen: view it and tap on the picture to click, with a Live mode
• Keyboard: type text (any language), arrows, Esc, Tab, Enter, shortcuts
• Media: volume, play/pause, next track, presentations
• Files: send photos, videos and documents to your laptop and get files back
• Clipboard: move text between your phone and laptop
• Status: CPU load, memory, disk, battery, network speed
• Power: lock, sleep, shut down now or on a timer

GET STARTED
1. Install the free DaSync app on your Windows laptop: https://syl1g.github.io/DaSync/
2. Open it, a QR code appears
3. Scan it in this app and approve the connection on the laptop
No laptop at hand? Try every screen in demo mode.

SECURITY
• The QR code is single-use and valid for two minutes
• Every new device must be approved on the laptop
• Per-phone permissions: control, screen, files, power (power is off by default)
• Every request is signed with a device key
• No data is sent to the developer or third parties
Note: traffic on your home network is not encrypted. Do not use DaSync on public Wi-Fi.

The DaSync app for Windows is required. Phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
